What I Offer

GxP documentation and radiopharmaceutical expertise, delivered with precision, on time, audit-ready.

Services

GxP Documentation

SOPs, work instructions, deviation reports, change controls, and Certificate of Analysis templates: written to pass review the first time.

Method Validation & Transfer

Validation protocols and reports for HPLC, GC, radiochemical, UV-Vis, FTIR, Karl Fischer, and osmolality methods. Feasibility studies, method transfers, and bridging packages, with validation to ICH Q2(R2).

Radiopharmaceutical Compliance

Radiochemical purity testing SOPs, radiation safety documentation, lab setup, and waste management procedures for radiopharmaceutical operations.

One of very few consultants with hands-on GMP radiochemistry lab management experience.

Instrument Qualification

IQ/OQ/PQ protocols and reports for HPLC and GC systems, dose calibrators, and radio-TLC scanners, for smaller labs that do not yet have their own qualification program.

GxP Training

Practical training for lab and QC teams: radiation safety, radioactive sample handling, radiation safety documentation, good documentation practice, and working to SOPs. I have trained lab staff throughout my career.

Typical situations

Where I can help.

  • A new radiochemistry lab needs its SOPs written before go-live.
  • A radio-HPLC method has to move to a new site or a new instrument.
  • A small lab has new instruments and no qualification program.
